Angelo Porrello is a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor (RTT) at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, Italy, where he is part of the AImageLab group, working in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision.

He earned his Master’s degree in Computer Engineering from the Department of Engineering “Enzo Ferrari”. He completed his Ph.D. in Deep Learning and Computer Vision at the same university.

His work focuses on efficient and decentralized learning, with particular emphasis on continual learning, federated learning, and techniques for modular deep learning.
